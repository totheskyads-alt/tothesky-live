What Claude does once it is connected

Claude reads your GA4 property and answers questions about your data: which channel brings first-time buyers and which one closes the sale, where your checkout funnel breaks, how many conversions GA4 sees compared with Google Ads on the same days, how much traffic stays unassigned. It can also check realtime data right after you change a tag. It doesn't change anything: the official Google connector is read-only.

The connector behind it

I use Google Analytics MCP, Google's official connector, free and open source. You set it up once: two APIs in Google Cloud, a sign-in with the account that can see your GA4 property, and one command that adds it to Claude. Read access to GA4 is all you need. The downloadable guide shows the exact steps and the traps I hit.

Why GA4 and Google Ads don't show the same number

Each tool credits a sale differently, Google Ads counts a conversion on the click date and GA4 on the event date. Visitors who refuse cookies show up differently, and GA4 needs 24 to 48 hours to process data. That's why I ask Claude for the reason behind a gap, not only the gap, and check one number by hand before I decide anything.

What it doesn't do, and where to be careful

Claude reads numbers fast, but it doesn't know what happens in your business: a drop can be a season, a sold-out product or a test. You decide that. And one trap in the setup: agency or Workspace accounts can block the personal sign-in. For many properties, the fix is a service account added as Viewer in GA4. The guide shows how.

In short

Connect the official, read-only connector once, then ask the questions you would otherwise answer with reports. Check one number by hand before you move budget.
