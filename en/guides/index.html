What is ROAS? How to calculate it, with a free calculator

ROAS tells you how much revenue attributed to ads you get for every RON spent on advertising: ROAS = revenue from ads / ad spend. Example: you spend 1,000 RON and generate 5,000 RON in revenue, giving you a ROAS of 5, or 5 RON for every RON spent, also expressed as 500%. Use the calculator to check whether your result covers your advertising costs, based on your margin.
