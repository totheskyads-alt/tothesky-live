How we work

How we work: Website creation

01

Free discovery call

We start with a free discussion and review. We look at what you sell, who is searching for you, and what website you have now. We tell you clearly what is worth keeping, what holds you back, and what your new business website should do to bring better enquiries.

Free
02

Structure and message

We define the pages, content order, and core messages. This includes your services, the areas you cover, trust points, and customer questions. For a presentation website, a clear structure matters just as much as the design.

03

Design and build

We build the website around your brand, with clean web design that is easy to use. We check how it looks on mobile, laptop, and tablet. For Transfer Confort Line in Switzerland, we built a premium multilingual website with WhatsApp bookings and GDPR-compliant measurement.

04

Launch and checks

Before launch, we test the pages, speed, forms, tracking, and basic SEO. For Eva Car Mats in Ireland, the old website had forms that sent nothing. We fixed the enquiries, tracking, and SEO so the website works the way it should.

FAQ

Questions about Website creation

The cost depends on what the website needs to do. The number of pages, languages, features, and whether we start from scratch or rebuild an existing site all matter. After the free discussion, you get a clear quote with no hidden costs. That way you know exactly what is included.

The timeline depends on the size of the project and how quickly we receive the materials. A simple presentation website moves faster than a multilingual site with many services and features. We do not promise a guaranteed deadline before seeing the details, but we give you a realistic view of the steps and their order.

Yes, you can edit the text yourself if we choose a structure that supports it. We show you simply where to log in, what you can change, and what is better not to touch without help. The goal is for you to update everyday information yourself: services, guide prices, opening hours, or the areas you cover.

Your Google positions need protecting when you change the website. We check the old pages and set up redirects, meaning correct forwarding from old addresses to new ones. That way Google and customers find the new pages. For a new SEO-optimized website, a proper move matters a lot.

Yes, there are usually normal costs for the domain and hosting. The domain is the website address, for example your company name. Hosting is where the website lives online. We explain the options plainly and choose what makes sense for a fast, secure, and easy-to-manage website.

Mobile speed matters because many customers decide within seconds whether to stay or leave. A slow website loses enquiries, especially when someone is quickly looking for a service. The tothesky.agency website was built by us and has a Google mobile speed score of 97-98 out of 100.
