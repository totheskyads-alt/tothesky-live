How we work

How we work: Growth strategy

01

Free 360° audit

We start with a free marketing audit of your campaigns, website, offer, and measurement. We check where money is leaking, which messages attract the wrong people, and whether tracking, meaning measuring key actions, correctly shows sales, leads, and payments.

Free
02

Clarify the direction

After the audit, we choose what deserves attention first. We do not fill the list with nice ideas. We separate what can grow sales from what only eats time. This gives you a marketing strategy tied to your business reality, not to trends.

03

Build the plan

We turn the direction into a clear marketing plan with actions, roles, and priorities. You know what we do in ads, what we change on the website, what we test in the offer, and what we track in reports. Every step has a reason.

04

Execute and adjust

We get to work together. We handle ads, the website, and measurement, while you bring the business context. We read results in sales, not empty impressions, and come to you first with adjustments when the data shows a problem or an opportunity.

FAQ

Questions about Growth strategy

The cost is set after the free audit, not before. We need to see what you already have, where money is leaking, and how much execution is needed. Sometimes the problem is in the ads, sometimes in the website, offer, or measurement. We tell you clearly what is worth doing and what is not worth paying for yet.

Early signals can appear quickly, but serious results cannot be guaranteed on a fixed timeline. They depend on budget, market, offer, website, and the data we have. We work with clear priorities and measure what matters for sales growth. If something does not move, we adjust based on data, not guesswork.

You get a clear picture of what is blocking your growth and what to do next. We show you the issues found in campaigns, website, offer, and measurement, then order the actions by impact. It is practical marketing consulting with usable recommendations, not a long report written to sound complicated.

We execute together: we handle the ads, website, and measurement. You stay involved in business decisions, the offer, and feedback from the field. With Flysmile, for example, tracking was blocked by a former developer and a campaign was running in Italy instead of Germany. We fixed the measurement and relaunched in the right market.

Yes, if you feel direction is missing or you cannot clearly see the link between marketing and sales. We can work as a strategic partner without breaking what already works. We review the plan, measurement, and priorities, then agree where we help directly and where we work with your current team.
