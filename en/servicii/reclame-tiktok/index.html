How we work

How we work: TikTok Ads

01

Free audit

We start with a free audit of your brand, audience, and the content you already have. We look at products, messages, social accounts, and existing videos. This shows whether TikTok Ads make sense right now and what should be tested first.

Free
02

Creative direction

We define the messaging angles, video ideas, and creator briefs. The message stays simple and close to how people actually use TikTok. The goal is UGC content, meaning videos filmed by real people, that sells without feeling forced.

03

TikTok Ads launch

We set up the campaigns, audiences, and TikTok ads based on the approved assets. We choose the objective that fits the stage: traffic, inquiries, or online sales. We track what happens after the click, so campaigns are not judged only by views.

04

Testing and scaling

After launch, we compare messages, creators, formats, and audiences. We stop what spends budget without healthy signals and shift budget toward the versions that gain traction. We tell you plainly what we see, what we change, and where it is worth investing next.
