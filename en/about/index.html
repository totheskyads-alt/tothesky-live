Who actually works on your account

We’re a young, dynamic team based in Malta. When you work with us, you speak directly with the people working on your account, without intermediaries. From discussing your business to talking through results, you know who’s on the other side of the conversation.

We work with Facebook, Instagram, Google and TikTok ads, as well as websites, email marketing and strategy. Working directly with us means you can discuss both an individual campaign and how it connects with your website or emails. Have questions about your account? You ask us directly.

Where we’ve worked and the results you can see

We’ve worked with clients in Romania, the Republic of Moldova, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Switzerland, Malta and the United States. Our projects include both sales-focused advertising and campaigns for enquiries and sign-ups. To give you a concrete picture, we have public results you can review.

For Regal Pet, Google Ads ROAS increased from 8.2x to around 13x. For Eva Car Mats, we generated over 1,000 enquiries at approximately €0.57 each. Expert English received enrolment requests from $6.67 each, while Matter of Care received family enquiries at around €15 each. These figures describe the results of those projects, without promising the same outcome for your business.

What we build and share

Alongside client projects, we’ve built Skylabely, our own tool for product feeds and custom labels in Google Shopping. The Skylabely Product Feed plugin is published on WordPress.org. It’s a concrete example of a product we’ve built that you can find publicly.

We also publish practical guides on connecting Claude, the AI assistant, to Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Clarity. These guides come from the setup we use at the agency. If you want to understand how we approach the technical side, you have two starting points: the product we’ve built and our explanations of the tools we work with. You can explore both before discussing your project with us.

How we work together, from audit to reporting

We start with a free audit before sending you a proposal. This gives you a starting point for discussing the collaboration before making a decision. If we work together, the advertising account stays in your name. It belongs to your business and stays that way.

Our reports focus on sales and enquiries, so you can follow results in terms that relate to your business. You discuss those figures and your campaigns directly with us. We don’t ask you to enter a long contract that ties you to the agency. An audit before the proposal, an account in your name and reporting on results form the basis of how we work together.
